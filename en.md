At registration, the user's first group is created. Records are kept separately by group. Each group has its own transactions, accounts, categories, currencies, and exchange rates. What you enter in one group does not appear in another.

The owner can invite another registered user by email. The invited user can accept or decline the invitation. After accepting, that user can see and edit this group's records. Only the owner can delete the group, invite people, and remove access.

If the owner removes a member's access, that person no longer sees the group. Transactions they already entered stay in the group.

When a group is deleted, its data disappears for every member. It cannot be brought back.

When an account is deleted, every group owned by that person is deleted, along with its data. In other people's groups, that person's transactions stay. The nickname is replaced with “deleted”, and they no longer have access to the group.

Deleted data cannot be restored.
